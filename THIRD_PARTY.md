## Quaternius Modular Character Outfits — Fantasy

- Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Use in this project: optimized male and female peasant outfit meshes and shared rig, recolored and adapted as a lightweight ancient Mediterranean cast. Original heads were replaced with project-specific low-poly heads and hairstyles.

The historical scene, heads, hairstyles, architecture, landscape, inscription texture, props, animation logic, interaction, and narrative treatment are original project work.
